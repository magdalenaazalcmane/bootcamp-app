# User can find a bug by title using quick search [Quick Search]

## Preconditions

At least one bug exists with a known title, e.g. "Export CSV fails on empty suite". User is on any page of the app, with no text field focused.

## Steps

1. Press Cmd+K to open quick search.
2. Type a word from the bug's title, e.g. "Export".
3. Wait for the search results to load.

## Expected result

A "Bugs" group appears in the results and includes the matching bug's title.

## Severity

Major
