# Clicking a suite result opens its detail page and closes quick search [Quick Search]

## Preconditions

A search returns at least one suite result.

## Steps

1. Open quick search and type a term that matches a known suite.
2. Wait for the results to load.
3. Click the suite's name in the "Test suites" group.

## Expected result

The app navigates to that suite's detail page and the quick search modal closes.

## Severity

Major
