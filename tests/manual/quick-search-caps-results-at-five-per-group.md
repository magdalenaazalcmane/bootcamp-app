# Quick search caps the test cases group at five results when more than five match [Quick Search]

## Preconditions

6 or more test cases exist whose titles contain the word "Checkout".

## Steps

1. Open quick search.
2. Type "Checkout".
3. Wait for the search results to load.

## Expected result

Only 5 test cases are listed in the "Test cases" group (the 5 most recently updated matches). No "show more" control or indication of additional matches is offered.

## Severity

Minor
