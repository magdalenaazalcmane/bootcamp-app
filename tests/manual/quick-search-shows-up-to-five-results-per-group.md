# Quick search shows up to five results in the test cases group [Quick Search]

## Preconditions

Exactly 5 test cases exist whose titles contain the word "Checkout".

## Steps

1. Open quick search.
2. Type "Checkout".
3. Wait for the search results to load.

## Expected result

All 5 matching test cases are listed in the "Test cases" group.

## Severity

Minor
