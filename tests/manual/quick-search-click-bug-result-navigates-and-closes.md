# Clicking a bug result opens its detail page and closes quick search [Quick Search]

## Preconditions

A search returns at least one bug result.

## Steps

1. Open quick search and type a term that matches a known bug.
2. Wait for the results to load.
3. Click the bug's title in the "Bugs" group.

## Expected result

The app navigates to that bug's detail page and the quick search modal closes.

## Severity

Major
