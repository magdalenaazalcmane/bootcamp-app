# Suite detail page shows name, feature and status [Test Suites]

## Preconditions

A suite exists with a known name, feature and status.

## Steps

1. Navigate to /test-suites.
2. Click the suite's name link.

## Expected result

The detail page at /test-suites/:id shows the suite's name as the heading, and "Feature: <feature> · Status: <status>" matching the suite's stored data.

## Severity

Major
